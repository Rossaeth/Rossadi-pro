Reflection
“You don’t lose when you fall. You only truly lose when you choose to stay down.”

This sentence carries a profound lesson about resilience and the true meaning of failure. In life, every person will fall at some point — fail, feel disappointed, be left behind, or lose something important. But falling is never the end of the journey; it is simply part of the process of growing up.

What determines whether we are defeated or not is not how hard we fall, but whether we are still willing to rise afterward. Many people give up not because they are incapable, but because they stop trying. Yet every wound and every fall carries a lesson that prepares us to stand stronger.

True strength does not belong to those who never fail, but to those who never stop getting back up. Every time you stand after falling, you are rewriting your story — that you were not defeated by circumstances, but forged by them.

So do not be afraid to fall. No pride is born without wounds, and no victory comes without the process of falling and rising. As long as you still have the courage to get back up, you have not lost.

“Failure is not the end — it is a sign that God is still giving you the chance to take a better step.”
